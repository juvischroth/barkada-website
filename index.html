About Your Hosts

Tom & Family.
Blue Ridge

We built Bärkada Cabin because we believe the best trips are the ones taken with your people. Bärkada is a Filipino word for your crew — your ride-or-dies, your family, your chosen ones.

We're a Filipino-American family based in Georgia, and we put everything into this cabin — the dual suites so parents get their peace, the arcade so the kids never want to leave, the hot tub so everyone ends the night right. We host the way we like to be hosted: generous, personal, and no detail too small.
