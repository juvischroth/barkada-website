♨️ Hot Tub Guide

Using the Hot Tub

The hot tub is heated and ready upon arrival. Temperature is pre-set to 102°F. Jets and lights are controlled by the panel on the side.

Rules

  • Shower before entering the hot tub
  • No glass in or around the hot tub
  • Max 6 people at a time
  • Children must be supervised at all times
  • Do not adjust the temperature settings

After Use

Please replace the cover after each use to maintain temperature and keep debris out. The cover lifter is on the left side.

Troubleshooting

If jets aren't working, check that the cover is fully removed and the panel shows "Ready." If issues persist, contact us directly.

🔥 BBQ Grill Guide

Location

The grill is on the main level wraparound deck, facing the backyard mountain views.

Starting the Grill

  • Open the lid before igniting
  • Turn the left burner knob to HIGH and press the igniter button
  • Once lit, adjust to desired temperature
  • Preheat for 10 minutes before placing food

After Grilling

  • Turn all burners off and close the gas valve
  • Brush the grates clean while still warm
  • Close the lid after it cools

Supplies

Grill brush, tongs, and spatula are in the cabinet below the grill. Propane tank is connected — let us know if it runs low.
